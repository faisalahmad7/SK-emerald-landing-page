Companion to [SEO-STRATEGY.md](SEO-STRATEGY.md). **Revised 13 Nov 2026 using free-tool data**: 1,409 Google Autocomplete phrases (India), sitemaps of 3 national competitors (~40,800 URLs), and heading benchmarks of ranking pages. ## 1. How topics are prioritised

Each candidate is scored on four things, all checkable:

| Factor | Source | What counts |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Demand** | Autocomplete evidence count in `cluster-plan.md` (later: Keyword Planner ranges, then Search Console) | ≥ 20 phrases high · 8-19 medium · < 8 low |
| **Gap** | Competitor sitemaps (`research/sitemaps/`) and outlines (`research/outlines/`) | Topic missing or thin at DesignCafe/HomeLane/Livspace = gap |
| **Business value** | Links to a money page that gets enquiries | Kitchens, sofas, wardrobes > generic tips |
| **Readiness** | Owner facts needed | Posts that need prices or service confirmation wait for them |

## 2. Rules for every page and post

- **Written for a Pune family deciding what to buy**: real sizes, materials, prices or price factors, and local context (flat sizes in new Kondhwa/Undri/Hadapsar societies, monsoon humidity, society lift rules).
- **Answer first.** Each section opens with a 1-2 sentence direct answer. Question-style H2/H3s taken from the Autocomplete evidence (e.g. "Is a modular kitchen good or bad?", "What comes in a modular kitchen?").
- **Specific and quotable.** Numbers, tables, named options. Write sentences like "At SK Emerald Interior's Kondhwa factory, …", never "best quality".
- **Honest.** No invented prices, timelines, warranties, awards or reviews. Business facts are confirmed by the owner first.
- **First-hand beats long.** The top "modular kitchen cost in Pune" page (NoBroker) runs ~3,950 words and 57 headings. Don't out-write it on length; win with the shop's own quotation examples, workshop photos and a direct answer at the top.
- **Own photos first.** Illustrative images only where labelled "design idea". Descriptive file names and alt text.
- **Author and dates** on every post: the owner once confirmed, until then "SK Emerald Interior team"; published and last-updated dates shown.
- **Length:** buying guides 1,300-2,000 words · money pages 700-1,000 · area pages ≥ 650 words, ≥ 60% unique.
- **Links:** every post → its cluster hub + 1-2 sibling posts; every hub → all its posts (matrix in `cluster-plan.json`). WhatsApp call to action with a pre-filled message naming the post.
- **Process:** every post follows [FREE-TOOLKIT.md](FREE-TOOLKIT.md) workflow A: brief → draft → `readability.mjs` PASS → owner fact check → markup → pre-deploy gate.
