Use BWP plywood where water reaches, such as the kitchen sink base and a bathroom vanity. In a dry bedroom, MR or BWR grade is usually enough for wardrobes, beds and TV units, as long as the edges are sealed. HDHMR is a good board for shutters, but it is not waterproof.

The board is the part you never see. It sits under the laminate, so two kitchens can look the same on day one and age very differently after a few monsoons. This guide explains each board in plain words. It also shows how to check what you are getting, and what to ask for in your quotation.

## BWP vs BWR vs MR: what do the grades mean?

They are grades of plywood, named for how well the glue holds when the board gets wet. MR is the lowest of the three and BWP is the highest.

Plywood is made from thin sheets of wood, called veneers. They are glued in layers, with the grain of each layer at right angles to the next. Two sheets can use the same wood. What changes from grade to grade is mainly the glue.

- **MR (moisture resistant).** Made for dry rooms inside the home. It copes with normal damp air, but not with water that sits on it. Shops often call it "commercial ply". Its glue is usually a urea type, which weakens with long contact with water.
- **BWR (boiling water resistant).** The middle grade. The glue is stronger and is tested in boiling water. It handles damp much better than MR, but it is water resistant, not waterproof.
- **BWP (boiling water proof).** Made with phenol glue, the strongest common type. The layers hold together even after long soaking. It costs more, so many people keep it for the wet spots.

In India, plywood for general use is covered by a BIS standard called IS 303, which defines the MR and BWR grades. Marine plywood has its own standard, IS 710.

## Is BWP the same as marine plywood?

Not always. Marine plywood is BWP board made to a stricter standard, IS 710.

That standard asks for set timber types, cleaner face veneers and a treatment against rot and insects. So all true marine ply is BWP, but not all BWP ply is marine. For most home kitchens, good BWP is enough. If a seller says "marine", ask whether the stamp on the sheet says IS 710.

## Plywood vs MDF vs HDHMR: what are the other boards?

These boards are made from wood fibre or chips pressed with resin, not from layers of veneer. They are flat and smooth, which makes them a good base for laminate and paint.

- **HDHMR (high density, high moisture resistant).** A dense fibre board with a very flat face. It is heavier than plywood. It resists steam and splashes well, which is why it is popular for kitchen shutters. Its makers call it moisture resistant, not waterproof. Cut edges must be sealed, and a slow leak behind it can still make the core swell.
- **MDF (medium density fibre board).** Its smooth face takes paint and grooves well, so it is common under PU paint. Plain MDF swells if it stays wet. Screws hold well in its face but less well in its edges.
- **Particle board.** Wood chips pressed with glue. It is the cheapest board and is common in ready-made flat-pack furniture. Screws can work loose with daily use, and it swells fast if water gets in.

Moisture-resistant versions of MDF and particle board exist. If one is offered, ask for its exact name in writing.

| MR plywood | Veneers with interior glue | Copes with damp air, not standing water | Wardrobes, beds and TV units in dry rooms | Sink base, bathroom vanity |
| BWR plywood | Veneers with stronger glue | Handles damp better than MR | Wall cabinets, wardrobes near a damp wall | Spots that get wet every day |

## Which plywood is best for a modular kitchen?

Use BWP for the sink base at the very least. Many people use it for all base cabinets.

The sink base is the hardest spot in any Pune flat. Pipes sweat, taps drip, and the floor in front of it is mopped every day. Wall cabinets stay drier, so BWR is common there. The hob area sees heat and steam more than standing water.

Some kitchens pair a plywood carcass with HDHMR shutters and drawer fronts. That works well if every edge is sealed. You can see how we plan kitchens on our [modular kitchen page](/interiors/modular-kitchen).

## Which plywood is best for wardrobes, beds and TV units?

In a dry room, MR or BWR is usually enough. Step up a grade where a wall gets damp.

![Wardrobe with open shelves, drawers and a sliding door](/images/18.webp "Open shelves and drawers: the board inside a wardrobe.")

Bedrooms are dry for most of the year. In the monsoon, though, an outside wall or a wall shared with a bathroom can stay damp for weeks. A wardrobe against that wall needs a better board, a small gap and a sealed back. Check the wall before you choose. A damp patch or flaking paint is a warning sign.

A bed takes weight, not water. Its frame and base panels need a firm board that holds screws well, and plywood does this better than particle board. A storage bed with a lift-up mechanism needs strong fixing points too. A TV unit is mostly dry, but it may carry a heavy TV and a lot of wiring.

## How does Pune's monsoon affect furniture board?

Damp air alone rarely ruins good board. Water reaching a bare edge does.

From June to September the air in Pune stays humid, and wood takes up moisture from the air. The real damage starts where water gets into the core. Common culprits are an unsealed edge, a hole cut for a pipe, or the base of a unit standing on a wet floor. Sealed edges, a raised plinth and a quick fix for any leak matter as much as the grade on the sheet.

## How to choose good plywood: check what you are getting

Look at the stamp, look at the edge, and get it in writing.

![Carpenter guiding a wooden board through a table saw](/images/blog-table-saw-board.webp "Check the grade stamp before the board is cut and covered.|Photo: Tima Miroshnichenko / Pexels")

- **Read the stamp.** Plywood made to IS 303 should be stamped near one corner. The stamp shows the maker, the year, the batch and the grade. Marine ply should name IS 710.
- **Look at a cut edge.** The layers should be even, with no gaps or hollow spots in the core.
- **Ask about thickness.** Sides, shelves and back panels are often different. Each one should be named.
- **See the sheets if you can.** Once laminate covers them, the stamp is gone.
- **Don't rely on a spoken grade.** If it is not on the sheet or the quotation, you can't check it later.

## What to ask for in your quotation

A clear quotation names the board for each part, not just "plywood".

- The board type and grade for the carcass, shelves, back panels and shutters
- Which units are BWP (at least the sink base)
- The thickness of each part
- The brand of board, if you want one named
- Edge banding on all cut edges, including hidden ones
- The hinges and drawer runners, and whether they are soft-close

## Frequently asked questions

### Is BWP better than BWR plywood?

For water, yes. BWP glue holds after long soaking, while BWR is water resistant rather than waterproof. For a dry bedroom, though, BWR is often all you need. Paying for BWP everywhere adds cost without much gain. Use it where water reaches, like the sink base, a vanity or cabinets near a balcony door.

### Which plywood is best for a modular kitchen?

BWP for the sink base, and ideally for all base cabinets, since they sit near water and are mopped around daily. BWR is common for wall cabinets, which stay drier. Many kitchens use HDHMR or MDF for shutters. Whatever you choose, ask for every cut edge to be sealed and for each grade to be written into the quotation.

### BWP or HDHMR: which is better?

They do different jobs. BWP plywood is the safer choice for carcasses in wet spots, such as the sink base, and its edges stand up better to water. HDHMR is dense and very flat, so it suits shutters and drawer fronts. It resists steam and splashes, but its makers call it moisture resistant, not waterproof, so keep it away from leaks.

### Which plywood is best for a bed?

A bed in a dry bedroom does not need waterproof board. It needs a firm board that holds screws well and won't sag under weight. MR or BWR plywood of a good thickness usually does the job. For a storage bed with a lift-up mechanism, ask how the fittings are fixed and what thickness the base panels are.

### What is the difference between plywood and MDF?

Plywood is layers of real wood veneer glued with the grain crossing, which makes it strong and good at holding screws. MDF is wood fibre pressed into a smooth, even sheet. MDF takes paint and grooves better, so it is often used for painted shutters. Plain MDF swells if it stays wet, so it is a poor choice under a sink.
