## How do size and seat count change the price?

More seats mean more frame, more foam and more fabric, so the price rises with seat count and length. A 3+2+1 set costs more than a 3+2 set, mainly because the single chair is a whole extra piece.

Arms add cost too. Each arm needs its own frame, foam and fabric. A 3+2+1 set has six arms. An L-shape usually has just two. The corner of an L-shape, though, needs a stronger and more complex frame.

Seat depth and back height matter too. A deep seat or a tall back uses more of every material. If you want a sofa that is extra deep for lounging, expect to pay for it.

## L-shape or 3+2: which costs more?

It depends on length and seat count, not on the layout name. A compact L-shape and a 3+2 set can be close in price. A long L-shape with a chaise end will usually cost more than either.

| | L-shape | 3+2 set |
|---|---|---|

| Floor space | Uses a corner well | Needs two walls or an open layout |
| Rearranging | Fixed to one corner shape | Easy to move around |
| Arms | Usually two | Four |
| Fit | Must match your corner closely | More forgiving |

The fairest way to compare is to ask for both layouts priced at the same seat count and fabric. Then the difference you see is the real difference.

## Frame and foam: the hidden part of the price

The frame and foam are where cheap sofas save money, and where good sofas spend it. You cannot see either one in a photo, which is why two sofas that look the same can be priced so far apart.

- **Frame.** Seasoned, treated wood costs more than fresh wood or board. Glued and screwed joints with corner blocks take longer to make than stapled ones.
- **Foam.** Denser foam costs more, and HR foam costs more than basic PU foam. A layered seat costs more than a single block.
- **Seat base.** Steel zig-zag springs or good webbing cost more than cheap straps.

A low price often means savings you cannot see: wet wood, thin foam or a weak base. These show up a year or two later, as creaks and a sagging seat. Our guide to [what's inside a good sofa](/blog/whats-inside-a-good-sofa) explains each part and how to check it.

## Why do recliners cost more?

Each reclining seat has a steel mechanism inside. Power models add a motor, wiring and controls. So a recliner sofa costs more than a fixed sofa of the same size.

- **Manual recliners** have one push-back or lever mechanism for each reclining seat.
- **Power recliners** add a motor for each seat, plus a power supply.
- **Power-lift recliners** need a stronger motor and a frame that lifts the whole chair.
- **Recliner sofas** cost more as more seats recline. On some, only the end seats recline.

## How to compare sofa quotes fairly

Compare quotes line by line for the same size and materials, not by the final total. A cheaper quote with missing details is not a like-for-like price.

Ask each seller to quote the same layout, the same size and the same type of fabric. If one quote is much lower, ask what is different inside. The answer is often the wood, the foam density or the fabric grade. Once you know that, you can decide whether the saving is worth it.

| What to compare | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Overall size and seat depth | A bigger sofa costs more for honest reasons |
| Frame material and joints | Decides how long the sofa lasts |
| Seat base | Springs, webbing or board change comfort |
| Foam type and density | Decides how the seat feels after a year |
| Fabric name, grade and rub count | The same look can wear very differently |
| Mechanism, for recliners | Manual and power differ a lot in price |
| Delivery, fitting and GST | Can change the total |
| Warranty | What happens if something fails |

### What to ask for in your quotation

- The size: overall width and depth, seat height and seat depth
- The frame: wood or board, and whether it is seasoned and treated
- The seat base and the foam type and density
- The fabric name or code, colour, and rub count if listed
- The mechanism, if it is a recliner
- What is included: delivery, fitting and GST
- Any warranty, and what it covers, in writing

If a quote leaves these blank, ask for them before you pay a deposit.
